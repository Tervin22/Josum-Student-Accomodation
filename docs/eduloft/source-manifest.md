## Verified Public Facts Used

- Eduloft is presented publicly as student living in Eco Park, Centurion, Gauteng.
- Public contact email: apply@eduloft.co.za.
- Public room categories used in this system: The Nook, The Studio, The Quarter, and The Loft.
- Public amenities used in the portal: private student rooms, facial biometric access control, 24-hour security, CCTV surveillance and analytics, free internet connectivity, smart metering, serviced laundromat, and generator support for essential services.
- Nearby places used in the portal include Gautrain Bus Stop at 0.4 km and Eco Boulevard Shopping Centre at 2.9 km.

## Conflicts And Open Items

- The Studio rate appears as R5 690 pp on the homepage, while one public room page copy has shown R4 990 p/m per person sharing. The app uses the homepage value and records this as a rate to confirm before production.
- The Nook appears as R6 250 pm on the live room page, while one brochure showed a different "from" value. The app uses the live room page value.
- Exact room count, bed count, unit numbering, deposits, cancellation terms, banking details, accreditation/funding rules, and storage fees were not verified from public sources. They are therefore not hard-coded.
- The Eduloft student portal page is password-protected, so no private operational rules were copied into this system.
